
# Video Game Sales Analysis – Jupyter Notebook Project
To analyse historical video game sales data to identify market trends, examine regional preferences, evaluate the performance of gaming platforms and genres, and investigate the contributions of major publishers to global sales.


## Analytical Objectives

- [ ] **Objective 1: Analyse Global Video Game Sales Trends Over Time**
  Examine how total global video game sales and the number of game releases have changed across different years.

- [ ] **Objective 2: Identify the Best-Performing Gaming Platforms**
  Determine which gaming platforms have generated the highest cumulative global sales and compare their market contributions.

- [ ] **Objective 3: Investigate the Popularity of Video Game Genres**
  Identify the genres with the highest global sales and compare their average sales performance per game.

- [ ] **Objective 4: Compare Regional Video Game Sales**
  Analyse differences in video game sales across North America, Europe, Japan and other regions.

- [ ] **Objective 5: Examine Regional Preferences for Video Game Genres**
  Determine which genres perform best in different geographical regions and identify differences in regional preferences.

- [ ] **Objective 6: Evaluate the Performance of Major Video Game Publishers**
  Identify the leading publishers based on global sales and compare their sales performance and game portfolios.

- [ ] **Objective 7: Identify the Best-Selling Video Games**
  Determine the top 10 or 20 video games based on global sales and investigate their platforms, genres and publishers.

- [ ] **Objective 8: Investigate the Relationship Between Gaming Platforms and Genres**
  Examine which video game genres generate the highest sales on different gaming platforms.

- [ ] **Objective 9: Analyse Changes in Genre Popularity Over Time**
  Investigate how the sales performance of different video game genres has changed over the years.

- [ ] **Objective 10: Examine the Distribution and Variability of Video Game Sales**
  Analyse the distribution, spread, skewness and outliers of regional and global video game sales.

- [ ] **Objective 11: Investigate Relationships Between Regional and Global Sales**
  Examine correlations between sales in different geographical regions and identify patterns in regional sales performance.

- [ ] **Objective 12: Identify Market Concentration Among Publishers and Platforms**
  Determine the proportion of global video game sales attributable to the leading publishers and gaming platforms.
